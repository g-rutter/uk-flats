Station highlights

  • Step-free category A: there is step-free access to all platforms

  • There are lifts

  • Toilets
  • Accessible toilets
  • Changing Places toilets
  • Baby changing

    • Monday to Friday: 05:15 - 00:00
    • Saturday: 05:15 - 00:00
    • Sunday: 07:00 - 00:30
  • Help Points are available inside and outside this station

    • Monday to Friday: 06:30 - 20:30
    • Saturday: 06:30 - 19:30
    • Sunday: 08:00 - 20:00

  • The platforms are above and below the main entrance of the station
  • There are tactile warnings on all platforms in use

  • 591 parking spaces
  • No accessible parking spaces

  • Drop-off / Pick-up point

  • Bicycle storage
  • Bicycle hire available
