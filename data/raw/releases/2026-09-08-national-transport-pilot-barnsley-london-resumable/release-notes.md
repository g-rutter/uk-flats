# Barnsley–London pilot capture

This release retains the four browser-visible searches needed to inspect the 10:00–14:00 Friday 11 September 2026 window from Barnsley (`BNY`) to the planner's `London (All Stations)` group (`182`). The shortest displayed suitable itinerary was reviewed across those captures and its expanded Journey Details retained.

This is review-only acquisition evidence. It does not update `nationalTransport.csv`, scoring or the public comparison.
