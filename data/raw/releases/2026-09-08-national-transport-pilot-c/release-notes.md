# National transport pilot captures C

Date: 2026-09-08

This release retains UI-only National Rail Journey Planner captures for the four
pilot origins Cardiff Central (CDF), Bangor (Gwynedd) (BNG), Reading (RDG) and
Wrexham General (WRX), each searched toward London (All Stations) and Birmingham
New Street (BHM). The planned journey date is 11 Sep 2026; searches cover the
visible 10:00, 11:00, 12:00 and 13:00 departure times where completed.

All eight route folders now contain four result PNGs, result accessibility
snapshots, form snapshots and capture metadata. Cardiff Central (CDF) to
Birmingham New Street (BHM) additionally retains expanded details for the
visibly selected 10:45--12:45 direct service, and Bangor (Gwynedd) (BNG) to
Birmingham New Street (BHM) retains expanded details for the visibly selected
13:16--15:57 one-change all-rail service. No itineraries were transcribed or
copied to canonical inputs. A reviewer must inspect the visible captures and
select suitable itineraries before any transcription.

The collector was run through the rendered browser UI only. No planner endpoints,
network inspection or machine-readable itinerary extraction was used.
